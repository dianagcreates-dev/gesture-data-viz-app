Free · Browser-based · No account needed

Present your data
with a wave of your hand

A gesture-controlled 3D data visualization tool. Turn a table of numbers into an interactive scene, then rotate, zoom, and step through it using nothing but your webcam and your hand.

Launch the appWorks best in Chrome or Edge, with a webcam

Gesture control

Point to rotate, open your palm to zoom, make a fist to reset, peace sign for the next slide, thumbs up for the last one. No mouse, no clicker.

Four 3D chart styles

Nebula, Galaxy, Starlight, and Neural — particle-based 3D visualizations that turn a plain table of numbers into something worth looking at.

Built for presenting

Multi-slide decks, custom titles, legends, callouts, and backgrounds — including a mesh-gradient editor for a background that's actually yours.

Runs in your browser

No install, no account, no upload. Hand tracking happens locally on your device — your webcam feed never leaves your computer.

Built with Three.js and MediaPipe Hands.
